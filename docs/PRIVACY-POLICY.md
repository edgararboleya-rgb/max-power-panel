# Privacy Policy — Max Power Panel

**Max Power Electrical Solutions, Inc.** · 6472 SW 132nd Street, Ocala, FL 34473 · Effective October 16, 2026

*Resumen en español al final.*

## Who we are and what this application is

Max Power Panel is the internal web application of Max Power Electrical Solutions, Inc., a licensed electrical
contractor in Florida. It is used by the company's owner and its employees to manage the company's own projects,
hours, materials, estimates and bookkeeping. It is **not offered to the public** and has no consumer users: the
financial accounts connected to it belong to the company itself.

## What data the application handles

- **Company financial data:** transactions and balances of the company's own bank and credit card accounts,
  imported through Plaid or from statement files; invoices, receipts, payments and the general ledger.
- **Operational data entered by the team:** projects, clients and job addresses, hours worked, materials, photos
  and notes about jobs.
- **Account data:** name, e-mail address and role of each person who signs in.

## How bank data is collected (Plaid)

The owner connects the company's own bank and credit card accounts through **Plaid**, a service that retrieves
account information from financial institutions with the account holder's permission. During connection, Plaid
shows its own consent screen and the owner enters the bank credentials directly with Plaid; **Max Power Panel never
sees or stores bank usernames or passwords**. Plaid then provides transaction history and balances, which the
application imports into the company's ledger. The only Plaid product used is Transactions (read-only). Plaid's
handling of data is described in Plaid's End User Privacy Policy at https://plaid.com/legal/#end-user-privacy-policy.

## How the data is used

Only to keep the company's books and run its operations: reconciling bank and card statements, recording expenses
and income, producing financial statements, tracking jobs, and preparing tax filings with the company's accountant.
Financial data is visible only to the owner.

## Sharing

We do **not sell, rent or share** personal or financial data with third parties for their own purposes. Data is
processed by the service providers that run the application on our behalf: Supabase (database and authentication,
United States), GitHub (hosting of the application files), Plaid (bank data connector) and the AI provider behind
the assistant features (which receives selected business data to answer questions, never bank credentials or Plaid
tokens). Data may also be shared with the company's accountant and when required by law.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or better) and at rest. Access is controlled by individual accounts and by
database-level permissions, so that financial data is restricted to the owner. Plaid access tokens are stored
encrypted on the server and are deleted when a connection is removed. Our Information Security Policy describes
these controls in more detail.

## Retention and deletion

Accounting records are kept for seven years after the fiscal year ends, as required for U.S. tax purposes.
Plaid connections can be disconnected at any time from the application or by contacting us; disconnecting deletes
the access token. Other data is kept while the company uses the application and can be deleted on request.
